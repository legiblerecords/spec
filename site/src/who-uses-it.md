Anyone can publish types under a domain they control. These organizations do, and their own sites document their types beside the schemas.

## Monorail Technology

[Monorail Technology](https://monorail.tech/) is a security consulting and software firm. It delivers the records it produces for its clients as Legible Records, and it is the first steward of Legible Records itself.

- [Legible Records at Monorail](https://monorail.tech/records/): what Legible Records means for Monorail's clients
- [Monorail types](https://monorail.tech/records/types/): every type under `tech.monorail.*`, with its fields and examples. Each schema is published at its `$id` under `https://monorail.tech/schemas/`.
- [Monorail data taxonomy](https://monorail.tech/records/data-taxonomy/): the kinds of data Monorail's types name, with its default level for each. Based on Fideslang by Ethyca.
- For language models: [llms.txt](https://monorail.tech/records/llms.txt)
